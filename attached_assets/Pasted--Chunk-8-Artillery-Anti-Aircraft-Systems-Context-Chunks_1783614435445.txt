 Chunk 8 — Artillery & Anti-Aircraft Systems

### Context
Chunks 1–7 complete. Full air combat system: planes, guns, bombs, guided missiles, Predator Strike.

This chunk adds **ground-based systems** — both anti-aircraft and ground-to-ground artillery.
All are **multi-block placeable structures** (a base block + facing direction + a targeting entity).

#### Artillery roster:
| Name | Type | Range | Damage | Fire rate | Targets |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AA Cannon | Anti-air | 80 blocks vertical | 8 HP to plane | 2/sec | Planes |
| Flak Battery | Anti-air | 120 blocks | 15 HP + 5-block burst | 1/2sec | Planes |
| MANPADS | Shoulder-fired item | 100 blocks | 20 HP | 1 shot + reload | Planes |
| Mortar | Ground-to-ground | 10–60 blocks (arc) | 6-block blast | Manual | Any |
| Howitzer | Ground-to-ground | 20–150 blocks | 10-block blast | Manual | Any |
| MLRS | Ground-to-ground | 30–120 blocks | 5-block each × 8 | 3-sec burst | Area |
| **Missile Command Truck** | **Mobile vehicle** | **150-block signal range** | **25 HP, AP 70** | **Manual, 2 tubes** | **Armored vehicles / any** |
| **Static Missile Battery** | **Placeable block** | **150-block signal range** | **25 HP, AP 70** | **Manual** | **Armored vehicles / any** |

#### AA auto-targeting logic:
AA Cannon and Flak Battery auto-target the nearest `BasePlaneEntity` within range.
They rotate (yaw/pitch) toward the target at a fixed turn rate — not instant (simulates real AA gun traversal).
Firing is automatic while a target is in range and within 30° of aim direction.

#### Armored vehicle targets:
Two NPC vehicle entities that are resistant to ordinary weapons and designed to be priority targets for the Missile Command Truck / Static Battery:

| Entity | Health | Armor DR | Speed | Threat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ArmoredTruckEntity` | 150 HP | DR 50 (light rounds ~24% damage, guided missile 100%) | 0.3 blocks/tick patrol | None (passive until attacked) |
| `TankEntity` | 250 HP | DR 80 (AP rounds ~60%, guided missile ~87.5% — requires multiple hits) | 0.2 blocks/tick patrol | Fires `TankShellEntity` at players within 30 blocks every 3 sec |

Both are non-craftable, spawn via creative spawn eggs or loot. They drop iron/gold blocks on death.

### Your Task

#### Create `BaseArtilleryBlock.java` at `com.airarsenal.block.artillery`
Extends `BlockHorizontal` (faces a direction on placement).
- Has a paired `TileEntity` that stores: current target entity ID, cooldown ticks, ammo count, yaw/pitch toward target
- Player right-clicks to configure (open simple GUI — or sneak+right-click to toggle auto/manual mode)
- All targeting logic lives in the TileEntity `update()` method (server side only — guard with `if (world.isRemote) return;`)
- **Do NOT spawn any external controller entity** — the TileEntity update loop is sufficient and simpler

#### Create `AACannonBlock.java` + `AACannonTileEntity.java`:
- `AACannonTileEntity.update()`:
  - Scan for `BasePlaneEntity` within 80-block sphere
  - If found: rotate toward it at 3°/tick (yaw and pitch)
  - If within 30° of aim: fire `AAShellEntity` every 10 ticks (2/sec)
- `AAShellEntity`: fast projectile (2 blocks/tick), on hit plane: deal 8 HP to `planeHealth`

#### Create `FlakBatteryBlock.java` + `FlakBatteryTileEntity.java`:
- Same targeting logic but range 120 blocks, turn rate 2°/tick
- Fires every 20 ticks (1/2sec)
- `FlakShellEntity`: detonates at target position even if it misses (proximity fuse at 5 blocks from target)
- Detonation: 5-block blast, deals 15 HP to any `BasePlaneEntity` within 5 blocks

#### Create `ItemMANPADS.java` at `com.airarsenal.item`:
- Shoulder-fired missile (held item, not a block)
- Right-click + hold: begins 40-tick lock-on animation (HUD shows locking ring)
- Release right-click after lock: fires `ManpadsEntity` (same guidance as Hellfire but slower — 2 blocks/tick)
- After firing: item goes into 200-tick cooldown (reload)
- Max range 100 blocks — self-destructs beyond that
- Non-craftable

#### Create `MortarBlock.java` + `MortarTileEntity.java`:
- Placed block with GUI: slider for angle (45°–85° elevation, controls range)
- Range formula: `R = v₀² × sin(2θ) / g` where v₀=1.5, g=0.05 (MC gravity constant)
  - This gives roughly 10–60 blocks depending on angle
- Player right-clicks with `ItemMortarShell` in hand to load + fire
- `MortarShellEntity`: follows parabolic arc using initial velocity vector from angle
- Impact: 6-block explosion

#### Create `HowitzerBlock.java` + `HowitzerTileEntity.java`:
- Two-player operation: one player sets target with `ItemLaserDesignator` (marks a `BlockPos`), one player fires
- Or: single player can operate via GUI (select coordinates manually)
- `HowitzerShellEntity`: high initial velocity (4 blocks/tick), long arc
- Impact: 10-block explosion

#### Create `MLRSBlock.java` + `MLRSTileEntity.java`:
- Player right-clicks with `ItemRocketPod` (stack of 8 rockets) to load
- On fire: launches all 8 `MLRSRocketEntity` within 3-second burst (every 5 ticks)
- Each rocket: random spread (±8 blocks from aim direction), 5-block impact blast
- 15-second reload after burst

#### Create `MissileTruckEntity.java` at `com.airarsenal.entity.vehicle`
Extends `Entity`. A drivable ground vehicle (4-wheel, moves on terrain).

Fields:
- `launchTubeCount = 2` (two guided missiles ready)
- `reloadTicks` (int) — 100-tick reload per tube after firing
- `controllingPlayer` (EntityPlayerMP) — whoever mounted it

Mount/dismount: same `processInitialInteract` pattern as planes.

Movement (server-side, in `onUpdate()`):
- Player W/S → move forward/back at up to 0.35 blocks/tick
- Player A/D → turn (yaw ±3°/tick)
- No pitch control — stays on ground, uses gravity

Firing (triggered by right-click / attack key while mounted):
- If `launchTubeCount > 0` and no reload pending:
  - Spawn `TruckGuidedMissileEntity` at truck's look vector from turret origin (top-centre of truck)
  - Decrement `launchTubeCount`; start `reloadTicks` countdown
  - Send `PacketGuidanceStart(int missileEntityId)` → client:
    - Client handler: `renderViewEntity = missile`, apply `predator_cam.json` shader (reuse Chunk 7 asset)
- Reload: `launchTubeCount` restores 1 per 100 ticks (can reload mid-guidance session)

HUD while mounted (draw with `RenderGameOverlayEvent.Post`, client only):
- Two tube indicators (filled/empty squares) top-right
- "SIGNAL RANGE: XXXm" readout — green ≤ 120 blocks, yellow 120–150, red (NO SIGNAL) > 150

#### Create `StaticMissileBatteryBlock.java` + `StaticMissileBatteryTileEntity.java` at `com.airarsenal.block.artillery`
Identical firing mechanic to `MissileTruckEntity` but placed as a block (like the Howitzer).
- Player right-clicks to sit; can rotate turret with A/D while seated
- Single launch tube, 80-tick reload
- Slightly shorter signal range: 120 blocks max control

#### Create `TruckGuidedMissileEntity.java` at `com.airarsenal.entity.projectile`
Extends `Entity`. The player-steerable missile for both vehicle platforms.

Fields:
- `controllerPlayer` (EntityPlayerMP)
- `launchPos` (BlockPos) — position where it was fired from
- `lifetimeTicks` (int), max 160
- `yawInput` (float), `pitchInput` (float)
- `topAttackMode` (boolean) — toggled by Shift while guiding

Guidance in `onUpdate()` (server-side):
- Apply `yawInput`/`pitchInput` smoothly (same clamped logic as `PredatorMissileEntity`)
- Move at 2.5 blocks/tick in facing direction
- **Signal range check**: if distance to `launchPos` > 150 blocks:
  - Stop reading inputs — missile flies straight at current heading
  - Send `PacketGuidanceLost` → client: overlay flashes "NO SIGNAL", HUD dims
- **Top-attack mode** (when `topAttackMode == true`):
  - For first 40 ticks: force missile to pitch upward +2°/tick regardless of input (climbs arc)
  - After 40 ticks: lock yaw, pitch downward to dive onto target from above
  - Effect: missile attacks from above, bypassing frontal armour — COD Javelin behaviour
- On block/entity hit: 6-block explosion, AP rating 70 (kills armoured targets reliably)
- At 160 ticks: self-destruct with small explosion

Packets — register IDs 6, 7, 8 in `ModNetwork.java` (IDs 1–2 from Chunk 5, IDs 3–5 from Chunk 7):
```java
INSTANCE.registerMessage(PacketGuidanceStart.Handler.class, PacketGuidanceStart.class, 6, Side.CLIENT);
INSTANCE.registerMessage(PacketGuidanceLost.Handler.class,  PacketGuidanceLost.class,  7, Side.CLIENT);
INSTANCE.registerMessage(PacketGuidanceEnd.Handler.class,   PacketGuidanceEnd.class,   8, Side.CLIENT);
// PacketMissileSteer (ID 5) is extended in-place — no new ID needed, just add topAttack field
```

Packet definitions:
- `PacketGuidanceStart(int missileEntityId)` — server → client: switches `renderViewEntity` to missile, applies `predator_cam.json` shader (reuse Chunk 7 asset)
- `PacketGuidanceLost` — server → client: signal lost overlay (`"NO SIGNAL"` flashes red on HUD)
- `PacketGuidanceEnd` — server → client: restores `renderViewEntity` to player, removes shader
- `PacketMissileSteer(float yaw, float pitch, boolean topAttack)` — client → server, every 2 ticks
  - **Extends** the Chunk 7 `PacketMissileSteer` (same discriminator ID 5) — add `topAttack` boolean field using `buf.writeBoolean` / `buf.readBoolean`
  - Chunk 7's Predator Strike handler reads only `yaw` and `pitch`; update it to also read (and ignore) the `topAttack` byte so the buffer doesn't desync

#### Create `ArmoredTruckEntity.java` at `com.airarsenal.entity.vehicle`
Extends `EntityCreature`. Passive NPC vehicle that patrols in a straight line and turns at walls.

- Health: 150 HP
- Armor DR: 50 — override `attackEntityFrom(DamageSource, float)` and replace the incoming amount with `DamageCalculator.calculateDamage(amount, incomingAP, 50)` where `incomingAP` is read from a custom `DamageSource` subclass `AirArsenalDamageSource(String name, int ap)`. Light rounds (AP 12): ~24% damage. AP rounds (AP 48): ~96%. Guided missile (AP 70): full damage (capped at 1.0)
- Movement: `0.3` blocks/tick forward, reverses direction on collision
- No attack AI
- Model: flat rectangular box (like an armoured personnel carrier), placeholder cuboid
- Drop: 4–8 iron ingots, chance of `ItemHeavyRound`
- Spawn egg: creative only

#### Create `TankEntity.java` at `com.airarsenal.entity.vehicle`
Extends `EntityCreature`. Hostile armoured vehicle.

- Health: 250 HP
- Armor DR: 80 — same `attackEntityFrom` override using `AirArsenalDamageSource`. AP rounds (AP 48): 60% damage. Guided missile (AP 70): 87.5% damage (min(70/80, 1.0) = 0.875 — tough but not invulnerable). Confirm this is intentional design: tanks require multiple missile hits to destroy.
- Movement: `0.2` blocks/tick patrol
- **Combat AI**: if any player is within 30 blocks → stop moving, rotate turret toward player (yaw only), fire `TankShellEntity` every 60 ticks
- `TankShellEntity`: 3 blocks/tick, 8-block explosion on impact, ignores entity armour (goes straight through)
- Drop: 2–4 gold blocks, guaranteed `ItemBrahMosTargeter`
- Spawn egg: creative only
